# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Atlas to be a project anyone can contribute to regardless of experience level, background, or how they found the repository. In the spirit of the project's own principle — every claim needs evidence, every fact gets a fair hearing before it's charted — contributors and maintainers alike are expected to treat each other's work the same way: engage with the substance, not the person.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on code, ideas, and decisions — never on people.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions before assuming bad intent.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any official communication channels) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers by opening a confidential report through GitHub (see `SECURITY.md` for the reporting contact — the same channel handles conduct reports, not only security issues, until a dedicated contact is established). Maintainers will review and respond to all reports, and will respect the reporter's confidentiality.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other project maintainers.
